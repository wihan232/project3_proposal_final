|     | Risk                                                   | Impact (1-5) | Probability (1-5) | Score (I * P) |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ | ----------------- | ------------- |

| 5   | Spending too much time on initial steps of the project | 4            | 3                 | 12            |

The highest scoring risks are tied between not finishing the project on time and spending too much time on initial steps of the project. Both are tied at 12 points. Second place is Other classes' finals conflict with the scheduling of this project. Its easy to see that these factors are correlated with each in terms of affecting the scheduling of this project. I need to use this knowledge as a precaution to not spent too much time on unnecessary tasks. The outlier factor is misunderstanding the project's expectations. I think I fairly rated the impact high, since not following expectations would lead to the project's failure. Because of the available resources, like having a rubric and asking my instructor, I believe that this risk is not probable.

#### TAME framework for dealing with Risk

Transfer and eliminate are not strategies that can be applied to the project. It's impossible to transfer the burden to another individual (unless asking the instructor for help counts as transferring risk). You also can't reduce the chances of risk to zero. Thus, the necessary strategies are mitigate and accept. 

Accepting the risk involves understanding the impact and probabilities of risk, which I have calculated earlier. Mitigation of risk involves following a planned schedule to minimize the chances of falling behind on the project. The table below represents the schedule I will use for the project.
