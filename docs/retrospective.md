
### What Went Well
I believe the final iteration of the webpage is acceptable in terms of looking professional and feeling responsive. The content reflects accurate information obtained from credible sources. I was able to create more than enough content to inform potential users about the topic that I picked. 



### What Went Wrong
I initially began this project feeling optimistic about my abilities to create a multi-layered and feature-rich project. I spent more time on brainstorming features than I did practicing the fundamental steps that were taught at the beginning of this course. As a result, I had regressed a decent bit before I began this project. 



### What I would do Differently
If I had the chance to do this project again someday, I wouldn't begin task unless I clearly defined it and set clear expectations for myself. Spending too much time on researching my topic didn't meaningfully translate into making a better website. I should have spent more time practicing the concepts introduced in the course in my free time.
