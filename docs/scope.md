| **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| This webpage will be based on a real life hobby of mine, which is learning about how logical fallacies appear in real life rhetoric and debates. Of course, covering every single logical fallacy that exists likely exceeds the time resources allowed for this project. Thus, I will create a webpage covering what I believe to be the most important logical fallacy, the Ad-Hominem argument.<br><br>The webpage will consist of five pages that cover one detail of the Ad-hominem argument, with APA citations. The content of the pages are:<br>1. A brief concept of the Ad-hominem (Main Page)<br>2. History and Origin of the Ad-hominem<br>3. Sub-categories of Ad-hominem<br>4. Whether Ad-hominem Usage is Justified<br>5. Modern Examples of Ad-hominem Usage<br><br>Each page will have an HTML file and style.css file. The pages will be interconnected, with specific words having hyperlinks that take the user to one of the other pages, similar to how Wikipedia works. |
