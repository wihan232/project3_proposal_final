A Brief Introduction to the Ad-Hominem Fallacy

Concept of Ad-Hominem

The Ad-Hominem fallacy, argument, or attack refers to a personal attack against a proponent of an argument rather than refuting the argument itself. Even in modern day, the Ad-hominem attack has seen no shortage of its practice.

Fallacies of Irrelevance

The primary goal of the ad-hominem argument is to undermine the credibility of the person making the argument. By drawing attention to the individual’s alleged bad qualities, the opponent can often avoid addressing the issue.

Informal Fallacies

The Ad-Hominem fallacy also falls into the category of fallacies known as informal fallacies. While formal fallacies resemble formally valid arguments, informal fallacies do not follow a structure of logical inference.
